# Ember audit 001, 13 September 2026

Mode: after (audit of the finished app). Method: used the app at `http://localhost:8765` in Chrome on desktop and at 375 px and 280 px, in both themes, from a fresh install, then with a seeded two-year history (729 days, six to nine tasks a day) and fifty tasks for today. Contrast ratios computed with the antislop checker. Nothing in the repo was changed.

Priority: MUST = fails a hard gate or loses data. SHOULD = falls short of the stated standard (hyper-minimal, luxurious, lightweight, useful). COULD = polish.

Each finding: what and where, the standard it falls short of, the smallest fix.

## MUST

### 1. "Clear done" erases today's record and can end the streak

- Where: handler at `index.html:1513`, control at `index.html:644`.
- What: it filters finished tasks out of the day. With everything done, the day became empty, the flame went out, the streak dropped from 1 to 0, and History lost the day. It is also a second path to what the per-row cross already does.
- Standard: useful, no data loss; one path per result.
- Fix: delete the control, its handler, and the `.foot` styles.

### 2. Midnight in an open tab breaks adding

- Where: `ensureToday` runs only on load and on `visibilitychange` (`index.html:1520`); `todayTasks` at `index.html:826`.
- What: with the tab left visible past midnight, today's list does not exist, Enter throws `TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'push')`, and the typed text is gone. Reproduced by shifting the page clock forward a day.
- Standard: useful (R-27, the app has no state for this).
- Fix: make `todayTasks` roll the day when today's list is missing, and add a timer to the next midnight that does what `visibilitychange` does.

### 3. Finishing or removing a task by keyboard drops focus to the page body

- Where: `renderList` at `index.html:983` rebuilds the list; `toggleTask` at `index.html:1389`; the remove handlers.
- What: after Enter on a task, focus is gone and Tab restarts from the top of the page. The README claims the next stop is the remove button.
- Standard: R-32 keyboard accessibility.
- Fix: after the render in the toggle and remove handlers, focus the row's toggle by id, or the next row's when one was removed.

### 4. "Add for tomorrow" and the calendar fail contrast

- Where: `opacity: 0.6` at `index.html:243` and `index.html:258`.
- What: muted text at 60% opacity on paper.

  | Theme | Ratio | Bar |
  |---|---|---|
  | Light | 2.42:1 | 4.5:1 text, 3:1 icon |
  | Dark | 3.2:1 | 4.5:1 text, 3:1 icon |

- Standard: R-25 hard gate.
- Fix: delete the two opacity rules and the reveal rule tied to `:not(:placeholder-shown)`. Muted at full strength is 5.28:1 light and 6.82:1 dark.

### 5. Paper text on ember fails in the light theme

- Where: `--ember: #c9501c` at `index.html:39`; used by Erase everything, Replace, and the day-row hover text.
- What: 4.23:1 at 14 px text. Dark is 7.2:1 and passes.
- Standard: R-25.
- Fix: light ember to `#bf4a1a`, which passes at 4.67:1 and barely changes the flame.

### 6. The top of Today says the streak four times, with two different numbers

- Where: slogan, streak line, date hint at `index.html:633`, foot at `index.html:644`.
- What: "Don't let it go out." then "42-day streak. 53 tasks left to keep it." then "Sunday, 13 September Day 43 of your streak." then "53 left". The 42 and the 43 sit three lines apart and read as a contradiction. The empty state is three messages too: "No streak yet", "A clean page.", "Your day is clear".
- Standard: hyper-minimal. The bar is the box, the tap, and the streak (C-3).
- Fix: remove the date hint and the foot. Keep flame, slogan, numeral, one streak line, and the date alone. Drop "Your day is clear" as well; an empty list under a box is clear enough.

## SHOULD

### 7. Three ways to put a task on tomorrow

- Where: the link, Cmd+Enter, and the calendar at `index.html:635` to 641.
- What: on a phone the two controls take 45% of the input row. In Chrome the hidden date input is three Tab stops, Enter opens the native picker, and Tab is then trapped until Escape. At 280 px the placeholder truncates despite the last commit.
- Standard: one path per result; an addition has to clear a higher bar. The any-day picker is the addition and the one with the keyboard trouble.
- Fix: remove the calendar label and its two handlers; keep the link and the shortcut. If any day must stay, it replaces the link rather than sitting beside it.

### 8. The consent dialog blocks first use

- Where: dialog at `index.html:712`, `askConsent` at `index.html:1378`.
- What: first open is a modal before you can type.
- Standard: useful and minimal. DESIGN's reason is privacy, but the storage is on-device and is the thing the person asked for. Nothing leaves the device either way, so usefulness should win.
- Fix: drop the dialog, save by default, keep Stop saving and Erase in Settings and the storage-blocked note.

### 9. Two theme controls, and a tagline inside the heading

- Where: header button at `index.html:599`, tagline at `index.html:598`, theme group in Settings.
- What: the header link and the Settings group do the same thing, and the header is the only place a lone underlined word appears. The tagline is marketing copy inside the h1, so a screen reader hears "Ember Keep the fire within" as the page heading.
- Standard: duplicate path; luxury (reads as a web page rather than an object).
- Fix: remove the header button and the tagline; the manifest and README keep the line. The wordmark is then alone on its row and the flame already says what the app is, so consider dropping the row.

### 10. History lists the same 35 days twice

- Where: grid and rows in `renderHistory`, keys at `index.html:1132`.
- What: both cover the same five weeks, both open the same day page, and each bar repeats its "9 of 9". With a long streak the screen is 35 ember dots over 30 ember bars.
- Standard: minimal; one accent at the key moment.
- Fix: start the rows where the grid ends (filter to days older than 34), and drop the bar, keeping the tally.

### 11. Settings shows state nobody needs

- Where: `<dl class="facts">` at `index.html:695`, footnote at `index.html:708`, lead above them.
- What: "On, since Fri 13 Sep 2024" wraps into two ragged lines at 375 px. "Space used" and "Days recorded" are diagnostics. The lead and the footnote repeat the consent text a third and fourth time.
- Standard: hyper-minimal; copy no longer than needed.
- Fix: delete the list and the footnote; the Stop or Start saving link already states the state. Cut the lead to one sentence.

### 12. A wrapped task strikes through one line only, and its mark floats mid-row

- Where: `.text::after` at `index.html:323`; `.toggle` align-items.
- What: the pseudo-element spans one line box, so on a three-line task the line crosses the middle line only. The circle sits mid-row instead of on the first line.
- Standard: luxury, execution.
- Fix: `text-decoration: line-through` with a thickness on `li.done .text`, drop the pseudo-element. Align the mark to the first line with `align-items: flex-start` and a top margin.

### 13. "Add for tomorrow" gives no visible confirmation

- Where: `#ahead-toggle` and `#list-ahead` at `index.html:648`.
- What: the box clears and the only feedback is the waiting line under the list, below the fold with a long list. A person hesitates and adds it again.
- Standard: useful.
- Fix: move the toggle and list directly under the input row.

## COULD

### 14. The nav shifts 4 px when the numeral first appears

- Where: `.streak-line` at `index.html:102`.
- Fix: min-height equal to the numeral's line.

### 15. First visit flashes Copperplate, then Federo

- Where: `font-display: swap` at `index.html:25` and `index.html:32`.
- Fix: preload the two woff2 files in the head.

### 16. The offline cache holds the page twice

- Where: `sw.js:4` caches both `./` and `./index.html`; the fetch handler only ever matches the second. 64 KB of the 176 KB cache.
- Fix: drop `./` from the file list.

### 17. Manifest theme colour is ember, page theme colour is paper

- Where: `manifest.webmanifest`.
- What: an installed Android app gets an orange title bar the site never shows.
- Fix: paper in the manifest.

### 18. Fuel decorates every row with the flame mark and explains itself twice

- Where: `fuelRow` at `index.html:1039`, lead at `index.html:653`.
- What: every row is Fuel, so the mark says nothing (R-04 purpose test). The lead is two sentences with one meaning.
- Fix: no mark in the Fuel rows; one sentence.

### 19. The accent strays past DESIGN's list

- Where: selected theme choice border at `index.html:397`, day-row hover at `index.html:537`, the bars, the partial-day ring at `index.html:520`.
- Standard: one deliberate accent; DESIGN says "nowhere else".
- Fix: ink border for the selected choice, no hover colour, and align DESIGN or the code for the rest.

### 20. The docs drift from the app

- Where: `DESIGN.md`, `README.md`.
- What: five sparks in one place and three in another; "never pulses" beside the flicker override; "the active view's underline" for a filled segment; the README's keyboard claim (see 3).
- Fix: edit the two docs.

### 21. The lit flame's glow is a filter on an element whose children animate forever

- Where: `.flame.lit` at `index.html:120`.
- What: the drop-shadow is re-rasterised every frame while lit. Not measured; the cost is compositor-side.
- Fix: put the glow on a static sibling behind the svg so the flicker moves transforms only.

### 22. The iOS keyboard key says Done

- Where: `enterkeyhint="done"` at `index.html:635`.
- What: reads as "close the keyboard", but the key adds a task and keeps the keyboard up.
- Fix: remove the attribute.

### 23. Task buttons announce their state twice

- Where: `index.html:956`.
- What: "Finished: Walk" plus `aria-pressed="true"`.
- Fix: label is the text alone; keep `aria-pressed`.

## Measured

| Slow 3G, from the byte counts | about two seconds to a styled page |

## Passes

- No horizontal overflow at 375 px or 280 px.
- Both themes hold on every view (R-34).
- Dialogs close on Escape; Erase confirms before acting (R-26).
- No console errors in normal use.
- No em dashes in the app or the docs (R-02).
- Storage is bounded at 730 days; save and render cost stay flat with two years of data.

## Not verified

- Installed iOS behaviour and the safe-area bottom bar.
- Import, since the browser pane cannot upload a file; the validation code was read instead.
- Reduced-motion; the code disables every animation and transition under the media query.
